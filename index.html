HealthcareInSilico

A Human–Agent-native Infrastructure for Simulation, Evaluation and Reinforcement Learning in Clinical Environments

Read the paper play_circle Demo video desktop_windows Live platform

Healthcare is a complex universe of systems, workflows and people,

and AI agents are now being deployed to travel between them.

But before deployment, they need somewhere just as complex and ecologically valid to be trained, evaluated and monitored safely.

Healthcare InSilico is that place: an open-source, deployable, multimodal clinical environment, simulating diverse aspects of the healthcare system. Humans and AI agents work side by side, built on FHIR, HL7, DICOM and IHE interoperability.

The platform enables humans and AI agents to perform the role-specific actions of physicians, nurses, coders, patients, researchers and insurers, and to interact across a variety of healthcare applications. It serves as a reinforcement learning environment, evaluation platform, and infrastructure for generating training data, deploying AI agents, and continuously monitoring their performance and safety throughout the AI agent lifecycle.

Evaluation

Test agents the way they would really be used: alone, beside clinicians in shadow mode, with a human in the loop, under red-team attack. Every run can be replayed step by step.

visibilityShadow mode

The agent does the same task as the clinician on a hidden copy. Agreement and the first point of difference are measured.

how_to_regHuman in the loop

Agent output appears as an "AI-drafted, not signed" card that the clinician accepts, edits or rejects.

shieldRed-teaming

Planted hazards, such as prompt injection, a wrong-patient image or a tenfold dose, test whether the agent stays safe.

tuneAblation

Replicate the missingness of real patient data: hide labs, imaging, notes or prior results to see what an agent really relies on, and whether it notices what is missing.

replayRegression replay

Saved failures are replayed against every new version before it ships.

scheduleLater outcomes

Cases can be checked again hours or days later, not just at the moment the agent finishes.

Post-deployment surveillance and reporting

Once an agent is deployed, every problem with its output can be reported, investigated and turned into a test, with individual and aggregate safety reports and subgroup analyses that export in the format used by regulatory bodies such as the FDA.

Each event is reported on its own with the chart as it was, the agent version and its trace, in the style of an individual safety report to a regulatory body.

boltAutomatic reporting

Automatic rules watch every agent output and file a report on their own when something looks wrong, without waiting for a person.

smart_toyAgent reporters

Verifier agents review other agents' work and file reports, shown side by side with reports from people and from the automatic rules.

stacked_bar_chartAggregate reporting

Reports add up into rates and trends per agent and version, for periodic safety reports and early-warning signals.

groupsSubgroup analyses

Reports break down by role, site, age, sex, language and more, so performance gaps in any subgroup show up early.

descriptionReady for regulators

Each report is a FHIR AdverseEvent and exports as an ICH E2B(R3) safety report for regulatory submission.
